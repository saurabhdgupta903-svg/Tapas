Our Story & Purpose

About TAPAS

A student-led meditation and wellness community at Pillai College of Engineering dedicated to mindful living, inner balance, and personal growth.

Who We Are

Creating Space to Breathe in College Life

Engineering studies can often be demanding, fast-paced, and screen-heavy. TAPAS was formed by students at Pillai College of Engineering (PCE) to provide a grounded, judgment-free space where anyone can slow down and discover the benefits of regular meditation and relaxation.

Affiliated with Heartfulness practices, we focus on simple heart-centered relaxation techniques that do not require prior experience, complex philosophies, or special equipment — just an open mind and a few quiet minutes.

Our Approach:

  • ✓ Approachable & Practical: Simple 15–20 minute sessions tailored for busy college schedules.
  • ✓ Open to Everyone: Students and faculty from all departments and years are warmly welcome.
  • ✓ Community-Driven: Organized by fellow students who care about mental wellness and authentic connections.
👁️

Our Vision

To foster a mindful, resilient, and compassionate campus culture at Pillai College of Engineering where mental well-being and inner peace are recognized as foundational to personal and academic success.

🎯

Our Mission

To offer regular guided meditation sessions, stress-management workshops, and open peer circles that empower students with practical tools for emotional balance, deeper focus, and lasting positive habits.
