More Than Meditation

College life comes with excitement, deadlines, projects, and exams. TAPAS is a student-led initiative at Pillai College of Engineering designed to help you step back, take a breath, and build healthy mental habits.

We bring together students from all engineering branches to cultivate clarity and emotional well-being through practical, approachable sessions.

Read Our Story →

What Happens at TAPAS:

  • 🧘
    Meditation Sessions

    Simple, guided Heartfulness relaxation techniques that anyone can practice.

  • 🌿
    Wellness Activities

    Practical habits for stress relief, good posture, and restful sleep during exam weeks.

  • 💡
    Workshops & Masterclasses

    Interactive discussions on focus, managing distraction, and mental resilience.

  • 🤝
    Community & Personal Growth

    A supportive group of peers where you can speak openly, reflect, and make genuine friends.

Core Pillars

What We Do

Simple, practical ways we support wellness across the Pillai campus.

🧘

Meditation

Moments to slow down, breathe and reconnect. Learn simple techniques to quiet mental chatter.

🌿

Wellness

Activities that encourage balance and positive habits. Physical relaxation, posture, and mindful breaks.

🤝

Community

Opportunities to meet and connect with other students in a supportive, zero-judgment space.

🌱

Growth

Programs that encourage reflection and personal development to build resilience through your college years.
